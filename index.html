About Me

I'm a molecular biologist with computational expertise, passionate about understanding life through science. My PhD on telomere biology combined advanced molecular and imaging approaches (3D-SIM, proteomics) with custom computational pipelines in Python and R for image analysis, statistical modeling, computer vision and data integration. Proficient in Bash, Python, and R, I leverage computational approaches to develop reproducible analytical workflows and explore complex biological systems. I am convinced that integrating the strengths of wet-lab experimentation with the power of computational approaches will accelerate discoveries and enable transformative advances in biomedical research.
